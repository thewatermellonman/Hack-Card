## Hack Card!
I followed the hack club Jams Hack Card tutorial! I made a cute little business card!

<img width="1341" height="760" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d6a41b3d-f5f4-4607-b16d-cfb1f27a280b" />

I made it in EasyEDA, then uploaded it to JLCPCB.

The card features a controller, and nfc tag, a capacitor, an led, and a resistor so said led doesn't blow up.
The point of the card is so that when it goes near someone's phone, they get a popup link to my website!
I haven't actually ordered them (I'm broke), but when I do, I can't wait to use it to easily give people my site.
